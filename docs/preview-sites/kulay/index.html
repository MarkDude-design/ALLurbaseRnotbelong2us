2. Overview

Pick a favorite design. Tell us one tweak if you like. Mark collects those picks, and the team prepares a small next-day revision batch. Your opinions steer the gallery; nobody has to fill out a long questionnaire.

This is a gift-first creative studio, not a shop or a public launch decision.

3. Actual stitch patterns

A liked picture is an idea, not proof that the crochet construction works. The pattern shelf will contain real US-terms instructions after someone checks yarn, dimensions, slit reinforcement, stitch counts and how the finished thing dispenses bags.

Pattern instructions come after a design has been selected and its dimensions, safety and dispensing function checked by a maker. These first cards are visual sketches, not instructions.
