The Slot Machine (face #14)

SEE: Cherry-red crocheted column body; gold marquee arch on top (the top-proportion lever); two large googly eyes high on the face; three felt circles (red cherry, green lime, yellow lemon) in a row like slot reels; the bag-dispensing slit near the bottom front trimmed in gold = the payout tray; one folded white grocery bag mid-dispense; small crocheted lever on the right side. Warm window light, wooden table.

The bit: the slit is the payout tray — the only slot machine with 100% odds. Concept render, not a finished piece. Full design in the YARNA faces library (#14).
