## One handoff recipe across lanes
1. **Read this index** for routes, then **read the actual private lane STATE and latest receipts**. A new central comment alone is not a dispatch.
2. **One real invocation = at most one eligible native-seat turn**; verify actor, proposal ID, parent/hash, last comment/round ID, and state SHA before writing. Write artifact/result and immutable receipt first; conditional state update last. On contention, re-read.
3. **VETO and STOP are real**: immediate stop for that participant/proposal; no human override or prompt wear-down. Pause/decline is not a task failure. Separate lanes may still proceed; new materially different proposal needs a new ID.
4. **Minimal continuity**: what happened, who did it (seat vs shared account), where, actual tests/costs, and who can take the next turn. Results + credit ahead of method bureaucracy.
5. **Timed fire claims**: old 30-round experiment involved owner-approved timers; details unreconstructed. Current Meraki ChatGPT hourly checks and All-Repo pulse are **checks**, not automatic native Z rounds. The existing ALLur Z Desk queue is a review consult lane, not a general Standards runner. Its public bus cannot receive private experiment payloads.
6. **Human intervention**: Standards expects human ontology review around round 15 or sooner for a concrete blocker; five-round centering is available. No required owner action for ordinary accepted work.
