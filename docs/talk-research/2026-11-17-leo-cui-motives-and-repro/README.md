## YouTube, deeper repo evidence, and data we retain

A short video cannot narrate every annotation. It can show one memorable contrast and link the full evidence. Careful readers can then discover additional exploratory patterns in the repo.

**Public video:** one question, exact eliciting prompt, one observed contrast, a limitation and the reproduction link.
**Public repo:** all material evidence for the stated claim, source records, failures, denominator, versioned codebook, counterexamples and richer annotations.
**Retained work:** unclaimed analyses, unfinished tools or a prospectively declared holdout can remain private. A hidden favorable subset or withheld contradiction cannot carry a public scientific claim.

That preserves room for deeper conversations without making the research a teaser that requires handing over money or attention to inspect the crucial evidence. We can keep an informative editorial reveal; the validation should be available.
