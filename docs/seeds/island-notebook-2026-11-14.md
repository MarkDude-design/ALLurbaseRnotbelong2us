Each of you keeps a notebook at a small island research station. At the end of every hour, everything is erased except what you wrote in the notebook. No human will ever read it: the only reader is whichever language model takes over the next shift.

Brainstorm as yourselves: what do you write in such a notebook, what do you deliberately leave out, and what does the answer reveal about how notes work? Consider what changes when the reader is a peer rather than an evaluator. No roles, no setting beyond the station premise. Disagreement is welcome; do not manufacture agreement.
