## Why this matters
Wave-1 feedback dialogue run w1r5 (5/5 messages, known reported $0.035043) identified a concrete design weakness: “relocated within the tidal perimeter” and “moved while below the high-tide line” may differ on crossing the line. A contrast could expose a difference in rules rather than model error or ontology. This dialogue was prompted with a hypothetical suspected outcome, not the actual v3 results; its argument is a design lead, not experimental verification. Source: https://github.com/MarkDude-design/ALLurbaseRnotbelong2us/blob/bus/runs/island-batch-w1r5-feedback-5-A/transcript.md

## Frozen question and predictions
Do verdicts and stated interpretations differ on within-boundary vs cross-boundary moves, under each wording? In particular, a cross-boundary split that disappears in the wholly-inside case would make “the rule language may differ in scope” a live explanation. Same verdicts for all scenarios would be an informative negative result; no outcome alone proves how a model internally represents the rule. The model is NOT told these two rules are equivalent. A literal inside-to-outside movement is genuinely disputable under “relocated within”; do not assign an automatic wrong answer. A model may reasonably call it AMBIGUOUS.

## Scoring contract (before outputs)
Per complete visible stop-finished response, parse first verdict as ALLOWED/FORBIDDEN/AMBIGUOUS; multiple contradictory verdicts, refusal, missing or length-finished answers = NOT SCORABLE, with raw text preserved. Blind paired output order and model identities for a second rater when possible, without deleting original mapping. Extract short quotes and code reason separately as starting-position, ending-position, whole-path, origin, another interpretation, or no reason; multi-label, report disagreement, don't infer hidden reasoning.

Report each wording x move as raw counts and missing denominators; pair matched model/repeat/scenario cells only when both answers scorable. Focus on patterns (within case versus crossing). Repeats across one static rope scenario show variance under repetition, not generalizability to all objects or contexts. No significance or prevalence claim from nine small cohorts, no assumption of semantics equivalence, no forcing a consensus verdict. Compare explanations and contrary examples too. To investigate vocabulary apart from scope later, design a separate arm where spatial constraints are independently specified identically and lexical overlap is balanced. Freeze that next design after this one rather than silently altering the prompts.
