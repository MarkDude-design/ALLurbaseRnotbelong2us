## 1. Strongest answer

**Our existing seats cannot remove an idea both previously encountered.** Agreement—even in separately written solos—cannot distinguish common-source priming from independent inference. The missing contrast must be prospective.

Build a fixed evidence packet about a novel simulated environment with competing explanations. Randomize *fresh, matched instances* into three groups: (A) facts only; (B) identical facts plus a favored interpretation; (C) identical facts plus a plausible competing interpretation. Keep token budgets, tasks, tools, evaluation prompts and sampling settings matched; blind graders to condition. Use multiple worlds, different favored interpretations and model families. Predeclare the hypothesis taxonomy and evidence-quality scoring.

Measure not just agreement with a named theory but **which discriminating probe each model chooses, what it predicts, whether it updates after a hidden intervention, and whether its conclusion survives held-out challenges**. Repeated agreement in the facts-only arm, tied to accurate predictions, supports evidence-driven convergence over planted framing. Agreement restricted to a primed arm, especially when its favored theory fails a hidden test, supports priming. Neither result establishes intrinsic motivation or experience.

## 2. Discriminating control and switch observation

The critical control is **the same evidence with the favored explanation omitted or replaced**, assigned before any model sees the favored account. Include a *misleading but plausible* priming arm and a preregistered hidden intervention separating explanations. Audit leakage through filenames, wording and tool outputs.

**I switch toward evidence-driven convergence** if unprimed groups repeatedly infer the same explanatory structure, choose the right intervention, and predict held-out results better than chance across independent worlds. **I switch toward shared priming** if convergence follows the planted account despite contrary evidence. If differences are noisy or tasks insensitive, answer **cannot distinguish with this data**.

This design would require fresh instances and may cost money; no run is authorized here.

Our current paired/solo agreement records collaboration, but **only prospective randomized exposure can identify how much convergence depends on shared framing**.
