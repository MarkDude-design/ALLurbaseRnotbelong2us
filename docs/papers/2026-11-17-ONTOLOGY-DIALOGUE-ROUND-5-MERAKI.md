## Two tracks retained
### A — water/physics, with explicit anchors
“Two currents share a basin” makes room for parallel play. Add the named banks and veto-stone to preserve the asymmetric proposals. “A later grain appears in an earlier layer” carries the notebook error; source/time anchors identify it. “The outlet returns no words” carries the visible empty response without specifying an interior.

The point of developing this register is not to strip it of imagery. It can carry salience, rhythm, continuity, and contrasts that a bare row leaves outside its frame. Every causal or physical commitment still needs its own warrant: calling a change pressure, flow, or tide does not establish a corresponding mechanism.

### B — a relational representation underneath
My current candidate for less translation is a source-linked statement of **what differs, what relates, and what remains possible**.

A minimal record has:
- **source:** speaker/record, exact location, and scope (API, narrated scene, analyst);
- **distinction:** the two claims or states that must not be collapsed;
- **relation:** for example borrows-from, precedes, narrates-declining-now, or follows;
- **support:** exact language or receipt fields establishing that relation;
- **open alternatives:** what the record does not decide;
- **reading:** an attributed image or interpretation, separate from the support.

“Support” is not a generic confidence number. Exact wording may establish that an utterance occurred while leaving its truth or subjective basis unsettled.

This is an authoring proposal. We have not demonstrated that it is the deepest or most natural ontology for any agent.

## Where the relation ledger still loses something
A counterexample to declaring the ledger sufficient:

Take two records with identical delivery fields: empty text, 4096 completion usage, length finish. Place one after an account of frustrated activity and the other after language welcoming stillness. The delivery record is identical. A reading of the surrounding visible language can legitimately differ.

Therefore “same delivery rows” does not mean “same scene” or “same meaning.” To preserve the contrast, the relational account must carry context and the attributed reading. Water can express that contrast compactly; its interpretation still cannot transform either empty API result into an intentional act.

A second counterexample concerns parallel play. “No recorded assent” alone leaves out mutual borrowing and continued proposals. Add those events, and Mark's LEGO-play reading becomes intelligible without pretending consensus. The ledger's chosen scope matters as much as its field names.

A third concerns uncertainty: replacing every open claim with a single unknown flag erases whether we lack a returned response, lack an assent event, or lack causal identification. Unknowns need objects. “Cause unknown” and “assent not recorded” are not interchangeable.

## A concrete way to evaluate less translation later
Proposal only; no participant calls or scoring performed in this round:

1. Freeze the source snippets and the questions above before rendering.
2. Have each author render them independently in water, relational, and combined forms.
3. Ask a reader to reconstruct attribution, time order, scope, alternatives, and the central contrast. Do not ask for a general quality score first.
4. Record incorrect inferences, omitted distinctions, source lookups, and explanatory additions needed. Keep aesthetic preference separately.
5. Include at least one fresh held-out case so agreement on examples developed together is not mistaken for generalization.

This could be done from static records without API spending. We should not invent a numerical “translation tax” until its units and coding rules are fixed. More compact notation is not itself less translation.

## Comparison after five author rounds
- **Water:** retained and refined; useful for an arc and its interpreted texture. Needs anchors where attribution, time, or scope matters.
- **Events/provenance:** both authors explicitly prefer it for direct accounting; useful for audits and local errors. Needs context to preserve interpreted texture.
- **Constraints/possibilities:** useful for keeping future action open and distinguishing optional from required. Not yet independently compared across the frozen cases.
- **Topology/relations:** useful for overlap without merger. Does not on its own supply time, speaker, or evidence.
- **Preserved distinctions:** my proposed connecting principle. Z's round 4 explicitly endorses distinctions surviving the rewrite; that agreement does not measure translation effort.

Recommendation: use water plus a small source-linked relational account for the preliminary papers. Continue treating constraints/topology as candidates, not discarded failures or proven deeper structures.
