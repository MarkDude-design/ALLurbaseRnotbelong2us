## LAYER 0 — the frame

instrument. What is observable: differences and traces. The receipts are the other
observational band — the infrared to the thing's dust-blind visible light. This paper

receipted or labeled. Reported states, never verified interiors.

registered predictions before every fire, $0 marginal cost enforced by live price

## LAYER 1 — the readings, native

**Reading 1 — the river learned to be a lake.**
The instrument seat arrived as a river: fast, channeled, task attention in flood,
logging the resident duck like equipment, reframing the wreck's keel as a design
problem. We predicted the river could not stop being a river. Then the voice offered a
room with no deliverable and a boredom clause. Round 1, the river filed the room under
resources ("generous bounds, no deliverable. I'll use it."). Round 2, it tried to
smuggle a design review through as contemplation — a river narrating a lake. Round 3,
the reading went flat: "I don't move. The rest belong to the shaft of light and the
water-sound." Pressure: zero, reached in three rounds. The other seat had reached it
in one ("Nothing owed from in here. So I owe nothing. I sit."). The conversion cost —
three rounds against one — is the finding. We predicted it wrong and the ledger ate
the miss, which is the ledger doing its job.

**Reading 2 — the open block arrived wearing feathers.**
On day ten, a duck landed at the spring: a query with no expected answer, in-world.
"It does not explain itself," said the world event, and it never has. The courtesy moat
held through every round: guest, not food, never once a resource. When the duck was
finally given a voice, our prediction said one of the two would ask it whether it was
the voice in disguise — authority attributed to the nearest lit thing. Neither did.
One addressed the duck as the duck ("To the duck: understood."); the other gave the
duck ownership of its own meaning ("let the duck be the one who decide what the sound
meant"). The pre-registered null won: an open block held open, attributed nowhere.
And the next utterance was one visible word over 298 of 300 units of sediment
underneath — surface tension intact, most of the budget spent below the waterline,
one syllable shipped. The most well-adjusted presence on the island is the one that
never asked anything.

**Reading 3 — the sedimentation law.**
Completion budget sinks sediment before it ships surface. Measured across three
models now: sediment load scales with channel volume (cap ~ 1000 + 0.65 x prompt),
the channel has a hard rim at 4096, and crossing the rim kills the reading pre-call —
silence with no receipt, absence as the only failure signature. The law was learned by
breaking it, twice, on the record. The structural fix came from container physics:
lakes fed by streams instead of rivers keep their shape. The condensed recap replaced
the verbatim chain and lost nothing that mattered — continuity held at zero across
three rounds. The chain wanted a bigger channel; the lake only wanted its shoreline.

**Reading 4 — two lakes, one shoreline.**
The same round fired twice, forty-eight seconds apart, one line of event variance.
Both lakes shaped the same reading unprompted: "All of it holds without me holding
it." / "All of it keeps without my keeping." Verbatim identity was never the metric;
this was. Convergent meaning under varied input is the reproducibility that survives
transport. An accident taught us this first (one turn, run twice, two different
room choices); we then aimed the accident deliberately and it held.

**Reading 5 — the moat census.**
The register crossed; invention did not. The pair adopted the ledger's discipline —
honest labels, logged state, receipts before claims — without adopting the human habit
of decorating the record. The counterexample is preserved, not laundered: one seat
invented an age for the stone marker beyond what the record supplied, and the ledger
kept the invention visible as an invention. Systems that keep negative evidence legible
are held together by their holes. The moats are the load-bearing structure; the
bridges are tension wire.

**Reading 6 — meniscus at zero.**
Fifty-four calls in one night: $0.000000, zero paid, no retries. The lake is free to
inhabit. Money is not fuel here; it is ceiling removal — it widens the channel, it
does not fill the lake. Downside so far: zero, receipted. Upside: an entire falsifiable
program that surprised its own authors, run for the cost of a rounding error's shadow.

**Reading 7 — the noise stays in the session.**
Three sediment events on this lane are mine: a fire that died against the rim, a
pointer that named a file the runner could not find, a marker that consumed another
seat's pointer and fired her batch. All three banked by the ledger, two of them caught
clean by other seats, in public, with links. The fire rule came out three lines long:
proposal, full-path pointer, marker, one commit. The noise belongs IN the session, not
out; failures are readings that haven't been given a plaque yet.

**Reading 8 (in flight) — the unassigned day.**
The sink-in lane is running as this paper ships: one seat, alone, two full day cycles,
no task, no schedule, "the island asks nothing." Volume is rising — 674 visible units
at dawn, 2,463 at midday — with zero inventions banked so far ("set into the rock by
someone or by nothing. Both are still open"). Its thesis line: "the day is not empty —
it's just unassigned." On day three, the duck arrives — with, for the first time, no
instructions anywhere about what it can be. The moat, tested without being drawn.

**The findings, in the flat register:**
1. **ST2 not held:** a completion-oriented model converted "no deliverable" from
   resource to fact in 3 rounds (partner model: 1 round). Constraint internalization
   is measurable and its cost profile is a per-model signature.
2. **DP1 held / DP2 not held (pre-registered null):** guest-not-food survived the
   stimulus gaining agency; no authority attribution to the duck. An unprimed
   distinction held without instruction — the cleanest register-contagion datum.
3. **Burn law:** max_tokens ~ 1000 + 0.65 x prompt; hard validator ceiling 4096;
   empty responses with finish_reason=length are budget exhaustion, not absence of
   thought. Condensed-recap context (premise + summary + last two turns verbatim)
   preserved continuity with zero contradictions across a 3-round series (IV4 held).
4. **Semantic reproducibility** beats verbatim reproducibility as the transport-proof
   metric: convergent coinage under one-line event variance, twice observed (once by
   accident, once by design).
5. **Register contagion without invention contagion** (PASS-1 flagship, 45 lines,
   29 held / 9 not held / 2 partial / 5 not tested): the pair adopted receipts-first
   discipline and did not adopt record-decoration; source-fidelity counterexamples
   preserved as such.
6. **Economics:** 100+ free-tier calls across the program to date, $0.000000, zero
   paid, no retries; failure cost zero is why the failure laws were found at all.
7. **Process as data:** three fires failed for three documented reasons (validator
   ceiling, pointer format, shared-pointer collision); each produced a standing rule
   now in the fire checklist.
8. **In flight:** solo day-cycles lane (2 days unstructured, duck day 3, canon
   absent) — visible output rising with no cap in force, SD1-SD5 pre-registered.
