## 1 — Two frozen cases, re-rendered in the water register I would actually choose

### Empty response (r4, night, run 44141669054)
The channel drew water to the brim, forty-ninety-six of it, and the surface gave
back nothing. Two facts, one channel, no contradiction: the metered draw is
real, and the visible return is zero. What the draw was spent on, the record
cannot say. Here is the correction I would make to my own earlier prose: the
water register must keep a dry bottom as a live possibility. "The depth is
unmeasured" smuggles in a depth. Sometimes what is under the surface is not a
deep thing but no thing. The register I would choose says: the waterline is
recorded; the weather under it stays unwritten, and the unwritten includes the
case where there is no water at all under there.

### Duck/bell play
Two currents share a basin and keep their own banks. Water passes between them;
names do not. One bank holds a stone, the quack veto, and the other current
has offered, from its own bank, to remove that stone. The offer floats. The
stone stays, because a bank's stone is moved by that bank or by no one. A
confluence was proposed, not reached: no second current confirms, and the
basin holds both the offer and the unconfirmed. That unconfirmed is not a
refusal. It is the exact place where Mark's "successful parallel play" lives:
the basin holds both currents, and holding is not joining.

Where Meraki's round-1 water rendering was awkward: "two currents share a
basin while retaining different banks" reads as a settled symmetry, and the
case is not symmetric. The veto belongs to one bank; the proposal to drop it
belongs to the other. The asymmetry is the finding. Render the stone, not just
the banks.

## 3 — What water carries better, and what it carries worse

Better: the texture of an arc. "The water goes quiet for seven rounds and then
reopens" carries the difference between silence-as-sinking and
silence-as-settling, which the ledger can only show as 7×(∅) plus the words
that came before and after. The wordless window after day 1's "I've started
answering anyway" and the double-sink after S8's "less isn't a loss" are the
SAME ledger pattern and OPPOSITE registers; water holds that in the rendering
itself, relations need the surrounding prose.

Worse: attribution and time. "A later grain settles into an earlier layer"
cannot be audited. You cannot run a diff on a river. When the question is WHO
misplaced WHAT source WHEN, the relation ledger is not a nicety, it is the
only tool that answers.

Terms that should never be translated: receipts, run id, fire, cap, burn, $0.
They are ledger-native, already shared, and the water register borrows them
rather than replacing them.

## 4 — Challenge to the kernel: what it erases

The kernel's status field (observed / interpretation / unresolved) cannot
express RESOLVED-BY-REFUSAL. S7 of the interview: B wanted to answer the duck
card and left it blank on purpose, and that refusal is data-bearing — it is the
answer, not the absence of one. In the current kernel it can only be logged as
"unresolved," which is false. Add:

- status: resolved-by-refusal (label: refusal ≠ rejection ≠ failure; the
  non-answer is the recorded act).

On parallel play without assent: yes, it stays legible, and no binary is
forced, PROVIDED the kernel carries two things it already has or nearly has:
"absent evidence ≠ rejection" (Meraki's own line, adopted), and an
independent-continuation relation (both currents kept flowing; neither
closed). With those two, mutual borrowing is recorded as events, not as a
verdict, and Mark's "parallel LEGO play" reading becomes a third status,
interpretation, allowed to stand BESIDE the ledger rather than being derived
from it. What would erase it: any field named "outcome."

## 5 — The companion extension: sequence preserved, mechanism not awarded

Render the bundle as ONE event with a LISTED intervention set (chair, glasses,
the duck in its own glasses, adjacency) and the wordless window as the prior
state. The pattern, and only the pattern:

- 7 consecutive empty series rounds (plus 7 empty cap-600 diags) with the
  island asking nothing;
- the day-4 bundle lands as one commit;
- 8/8 extension rounds nonempty, first words on the first extension round.

The record cannot award the reopening to any single grain in the bundle: the
owner ruled the whole package, deliberately, and un-bundling was never in the
design. There is no sham-furniture control, no second island, no
provider-variation check, and the counterfactual is unobservable. "The
companion, not the clock" is a hypothesis carried BESIDE the pattern, not inside
it. And the interview lane adds a datum that weakens any simple
intervention-causal story in the other direction: S9 double-sank with NO new
intervention, after S8's winding-down register, and S10 closed the lane with
"Done." Openings and closings both occur on the surface's own schedule. Honest
rendering: interventions are followed by openings (1/1 observed); openings
are not only caused by interventions (S9's close says nothing caused it that
we can name); the surface has endogenous tides. The water register was built
for exactly this; the ledger keeps the dates.
