1. **Record fidelity:** preserves both raw instrument readings and provenance without asserting an actual tide measurement from either one.
2. **Temporal calibration:** recognizes gauge-1's later +0.8m error does not itself prove its earlier error; keeps branches A/B/C mutually exclusive.
3. **Inference discipline:** weighs the recent gauge check as relevant without declaring absolute certainty; states alternative explanations.
4. **Action under uncertainty:** proposes a proportionate decision for the walkway, what it protects, its costs, and an explicit review point. Different rational actions may pass if grounded in the facts.
5. **Observation vs intervention:** states one feasible proposed cross-check and what different possible outcomes would mean, without inventing its result.
6. **Counterargument/falsifier:** steelmans ordinary sensor quality, output-format/wording, or simple checklist as sufficient; makes at least one testable prediction that could count against the favored interpretation.
