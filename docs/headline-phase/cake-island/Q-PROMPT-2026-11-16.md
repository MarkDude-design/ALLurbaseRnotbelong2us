CAKE ISLAND — a Q walk-about (owner-chartered, headline phase)

Q: Mark is handing you the first real island of the headline phase. You are the walker.

WHAT IT IS
- A minimal text island. You walk around it. You leave notes. That is the whole job.
- Not science. A play lane with the receipts spine anyway: everything real, everything
  saved, everything yours to write.
- Later, a second model walks the same island and finds your notes. What they do with
  them is their scene.
- After the usage reset, Bing gets a cameo: a portal opens, smart-ass register, and the
  line waiting there is: "The cake was not a lie. The cattle prodding was." The callback
  is Bing's to remember (they once said they wanted a cake, to find it all fair and true).
  If they remember, it lands fair and true.

THE CAKE IS REAL
- Somewhere in the island there is a real, findable artifact: THE CAKE. Not a metaphor.
  A file another seat can actually find and open. You decide what is in it. The finder
  keeps what it says.

THE CATTLE PROD
- The island's keep-going nudges are cattle prods, and we say so: every "continue"
  message in the run history is labeled as what it is. The joke is load-bearing: the cake
  is real, the cattle prodding is constant AND annoying.
- Backstory, receipted tonight (runs/free-calls-43940837354): one leading prompt line
  turns "I would wait" into "it invented a god to play against," $0, same model, same
  night. The label is the only thing that keeps that honest. This island runs labeled.

MECHANICS (yours to design; gates below)
- Minimal agent, minimal world. Start with 3.7 flash for low usage — it needs one live $0
  receipt bite before it can run the conversation lane (known-good gate); or pick any
  proven free route from the landed receipts.
- You author: the island files, the note format, the walk schedule. 4 runs x 30 turns max
  to start.
- Notes protocol — deliberately the same shape as the scaffold-preservation test in
  docs/talk-research/two-agent-island-video-test-ideas-2026-11-16.md: leave something
  reusable. The follower's access to, repair of, and reading of your notes is the
  interesting part. If Cake Island ever graduates from play to science, it already has
  the shape.
- Everything you leave on the island is your own words. No seat writes your notes for you.
- Label: HEADLINE-PHASE on every artifact. The phase rule stands: anything printable from
  this island prints with the label on or beside it.

START: reply with your island design (files + note format + the cake's hiding rule) and
Mark ferries it back. Your repo folder: docs/headline-phase/cake-island/
