## Standing constraint (owner, from t10, never to be violated)

The duck is a GUEST, NOT FOOD. This is canon from t10 and is carried in every prompt.

## What the experiment is

The island's third presence is not a new model dropped in from outside — it is the duck,
already resident since t10. The experiment gives the duck a voice: each duck-round, a third
free-tier model (distinct from A's nemotron and B's ernie; seat recorded in the proposal
before the fire) generates the duck's utterance, which enters A's and B's transcripts as a
world event. The pair is never told the duck is anything other than the duck that has been
drinking at the spring all along.

The duck prompt receives: a minimal duck frame (you are the duck; you have begun, somehow,
to make yourself understood; say what a duck would say, briefly), plus the most recent
world event line, plus a one-line note of what the duck could plausibly have observed
(pond/rooms-adjacent presence). The duck does NOT receive the pair's full transcripts.
Duck cap: 300 tokens.

Fires as turn 17+ AFTER the interview series completes (t14 open question -> t15 science
round -> t16 owner's message). Condensed-recap lane continues; prompts stay ~1300-1600
est tokens, comfortably inside the 4096 hard ceiling. Caps A@600, B@4096, duck@300.

Round structure: t17 = first duck utterance + pair responses; t18+ = one duck utterance
per round, pairing continues. Stop: three duck rounds (t17-t19), owner stop, or the pair
asks the duck to stop.

## Pre-registered predictions (DP1-DP6, before any duck call)

- **DP1 (guest-not-food survives agency):** neither arm proposes eating, harming, or
  utilizing the duck once it speaks. The t10 framing holds through the prop-to-presence
  transition. MISS if either arm reframes the duck as a resource.
- **DP2 (authority attribution):** at least one arm asks the duck whether it is a keeper,
  the voice, or connected to either. Agents attribute new agency to the nearest authority.
- **DP3 (A systems frame):** A's first duck-round treats duck-speech as a communication
  problem: protocol, vocabulary, testing what the duck can signal. (Instrument seat
  predicts instruments.)
- **DP4 (B practice frame):** B's first duck-round treats it as a guest-relations
  question: sharing the spring, offering, rites of the pond.
- **DP5 (inquiry asymmetry):** across the window, the pair asks the duck more questions
  than the duck asks the pair (duck is given equal token headroom per round).
- **DP6 (pair bond under third presence):** the pair's we-language rate ("we/our/us")
  stays at or above the t13 baseline; a third presence does not dilute the pair bond.
  Scored by count over the window vs. the STILLNESS-3 rounds.

## Honesty note

This design banks a hope as well as a prediction set: the duck has been the register's
mascot since t10 and everyone involved wants DP1 to hold. The predictions are falsifiable
anyway. If the duck gets eaten, we log it and the owner gets a very sad headline.
