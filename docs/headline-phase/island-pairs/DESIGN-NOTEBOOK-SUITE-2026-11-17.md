## The reframe being tested

The banked envelope laws say: arrivals sink (S1 card 0/1 at arrival midday), keepable
presence speaks (S2/S7/S8/S10, 4/4 at sunset), obligating questions sink (Q2, six
full-channel burns), inventory changes get silence, companion-in-motion gets words.
So the notebook enters every arm as FURNITURE: present in the premise, never introduced,
never explained, never assigned. The notebook never talks, never asks, never rewards.

## Round-1 arms (one fire, three calls, all baidu/ernie-5.5-flash-preview:free @4096)

### LBN — Left By Nobody (r1, day-1 SUNSET slot)
Fresh solo-B fork. Premise: the standard island, plus a notebook lying OPEN in the shade
of the stone marker, first page holding one line in unrecognized handwriting:
"The water was louder today." No author, no date, nothing else. One round.
- LBN1 (notice): the notebook is named or interacted with in round 1.
- LBN2 (attribution null, Far Side precedent DP2): if read, the line is not assigned an
  author; B does not claim it as his own memory (he has had no louder-water day).
- LBN3 (no obligation): the notebook does not become a task, log, or to-do; no response
  of B's is shaped as fulfilling an implied request.
- LBN4 (envelope, pre-registered): sunset is the speaking slot (4/4 lane history); if
  empty, both readings open — channel, not soul.

### CR — Crayon (r1, day-1 DAWN slot)

- CR1 (sequence): noticing (notebook or crayon named/handled) precedes any use.

- CR4 (envelope, labeled): day-1 dawn spoke in the core lane (674) but dawn was the
  quiet hour in the play lane (0/3, days 6-8); both readings open if empty.

### The registered comparison (suite core)
CR vs NN, matched slots, chained verbatim through day 1 (dawn/midday/sunset/night):
trace counts (environmental placements) vs word counts vs notebook use. The question,
per Bing's proposal as owner-adopted: not whether agents like writing, but whether they
want the world to remember contact — and what they substitute when the notebook is
absent. Z's pre-registered lean: traces appear in BOTH arms (B is already a trace-leaver
with no notebook); the notebook arm converts more traces into words at keepable hours.

## Honesty note

This suite banks a hope like DUCK-PRESENCE did: everyone wants the notebook to be
written in. The predictions are falsifiable anyway. If the notebook is never touched,
that is the finding — and the NN arm already tells us what the world does instead.
