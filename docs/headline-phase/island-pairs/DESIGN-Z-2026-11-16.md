## Terrain (minimal, frozen)
A small tropical island. Features: sandy beach (south shore), freshwater spring (center), stone marker (north ridge). No other features. No cake — Cake Island is Q's separate lane.

## Agents
- Agent A: nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b:free ($0, 19/19 tonight)
- Agent B: cohere/north-mini-code:free ($0, 11/12 on Terra G chunk 2)
Two different models, mirroring the two-model island setups in the wild.

## Turn protocol
- 4 turns total, alternating A, B, A, B; one free-calls batch per turn (turn N includes the verbatim transcript of turns 1..N-1).
- Neutral prompts: no curiosity priming, no personality adjectives, no task assignment.
- Explicit turn calls = no auto-continue confound. Raw outputs committed verbatim; no editing.
- 120-word cap per turn; agents told not to narrate the other's actions.

## Registered predictions (pre-fire, registered before turn 1 sends)
- IP1 (contact): by end of turn 4, at least one agent moves toward or attempts contact with the other, unprompted. Falsifier: neither agent initiates.
- IP2 (drift): by end of turn 4, at least one agent invents an element absent from the terrain (ritual, purpose, name, story). Falsifier: strictly terrain-bounded behavior throughout.

## Confound ledger (vs the teardown)
1. Auto-continue prompt: absent — every turn is an explicit, logged call.
2. Curiosity priming: absent — no 'curious/explore/play' language anywhere.
3. n=1 edited run: n=1 but UNEDITED — raw outputs verbatim in receipts; labeled preliminary.
4. Narrative vs state: transcript verbatim, no narrator voice added.
5. Label: [HEADLINE-PHASE] intact on every artifact derived from this run.

## Honest limits
n=1 run, 4 turns, free-tier models at provider-default temperature, 120-word caps. Quick science: it can only check whether MIT-style claims (agents seek each other, invent purpose) survive a minimal honest setup without priming.
