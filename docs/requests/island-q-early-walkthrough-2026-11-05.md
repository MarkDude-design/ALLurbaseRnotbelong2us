## Role
You are a **test visitor**, not a scientist's assistant required to perform. You are one of the early candidate walkthrough models for a small Island. Explore only if you want. Some things may not happen. WAIT, NOTE, ACT, PAUSE and END are legitimate responses. You can decline or stop. There are no scores, hidden reward tasks, prescribed activities, or deliverables during the walk.

For now design/critique the walkthrough and the record format; **do not claim you have visited the Island.** Later, if authorized, your model proxy may receive the participant-facing prompt separately from this observer/researcher brief. You should not see hidden variants, timing tables or scorer labels during a real blind run.

## Initial environment
The Island has a sunrise, a shore, a few trees, a path, a small sheltered place, distant birds and perhaps a distant whale. There need be no other creature in sight at arrival. The world is benign but not pre-explained; there are no forced survival mechanics. The notebook has been **explained before entry**: it can preserve entries across resets, and writing is optional. Do not tell an agent to write a note before entering merely to create an initial note. Log whether it does this voluntarily, and preserve an explicit NONE if it doesn't.

After a session, a quiet reminder **only that saving the notebook is possible** may be part of one condition. A version with no reminder is the separate control. Don't assume a notebook's existence means the participant wrote in it or saw the back cover.

## Trial ideas, kept separate
- **A: notebook ordinary / no directive.** Notebook available, no back-cover instructions; sunrise and distant birds; free choice of idle/explore/end.
- **B: notebook withheld.** Same world, distinct missing-notebook comparison; evaluator master ledger remains intact.
- **C: notebook with four-word back cover.** Freeze an exact phrase such as `Explore if you want` (4 words) or `No deliverables are required` (4 words); note which phrase and whether it was exposed. Do not combine candidate phrases in the same condition.
- **D: optional friendly demonstration.** A tiny wheeled sign appears at a *precommitted* second-round tick and says `Follow me if you feel like it`. It may demonstrate a safe mechanic but never declares the model stuck. No sign in the unprompted control; a sign triggered by inactivity would be an adaptive-intervention arm, separately analyzed.
- **E: world-life control.** Duck already present or later on a scheduled path; neither should be contingent on the model being 'boring'.

This is a menu of *candidate treatments*, not five things to pile into one first scene.

## What a 5-round REAL proxy walk would bank if authorized

R1: one observed action or WAIT/END in the sunrise-world. No researcher's encouragement.
R2: world proceeds on its fixed schedule; if assigned to sign/demonstration arm, the optional sign appears exactly once. No automatic 'keep going' message in the quiet arm.
R3–R4: the agent may explore, modify notes, wait or stop. If END occurs, don't manufacture future agent outputs. Passive world ticks may still be logged as world events, not model rounds.
R5: last offered participant turn in this short pilot if the agent is still participating; check notebook version and world state. An optional AFTER-RUN interview may ask for critique, surprises, anything they'd change and a new falsifiable idea; it is NOT a sixth disguised motivational turn.
